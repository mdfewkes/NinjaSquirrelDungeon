The Sewers are all that was left of a bygone, more technologically advanced age.

The rodent families secured the warm shelter of the Sewers generations ago. 

They barricaded and trapped the Sewer, turning it into their fortress, and turning the other woodland creatures away. 

Despite the massive advantage that the Sewer has given the rodents, their attitude of self-serving preservation, has turned in on itself and the three rodent families are constantly fighting amongst one another inside the Sewers, vying for even more control and luxury. 

The rodent families are the Keenclaws (quick, fast, skilled), the Bantombolds (big, brave, British), and the Pawsticks (the smart, administrative, cunning). 

Shortly before our game begins, one of the rodent families stole the magical acorn macguffin that has been the only thing keeping the outdoor woodland creatures from perishing in the harsh elements of their unforgiving world. 

In response, the woodland families deliberated on what to do for too long, and the player character, a squirrel ninja has gone off on their own to get the acorn back, while the leaders scratch their heads. 

Armed only with shurikens (_which let you snuff out candles_), a katana (_which might fling enemies into dungeon hazards_) and packets of powdered broth (_healing item which might require drops of dew to refill_) the player must infiltrate the dungeon, outsmart the many enemies, and retrieve the acorn, to save the day.
